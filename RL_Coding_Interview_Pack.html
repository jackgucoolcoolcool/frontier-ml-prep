1 · Concepts you must know cold

ConceptThe one-sentence version you should say
MDP $(\mathcal{S},\mathcal{A},p,r,\gamma)$States, actions, transition kernel, reward, discount; the policy $\pi_\theta(a|s)$ is what we optimize.
Return / objective$G_t=\sum_{t'\ge t}\gamma^{t'-t}r_{t'}$; maximize $J(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim\pi_\theta}[G_0]$ — the expected return.
$V$, $Q$, advantage$V^\pi(s)=\mathbb{E}[G_t|s]$, $Q^\pi(s,a)=\mathbb{E}[G_t|s,a]$, $A=Q-V$ = "how much better than the policy's average."
On- vs off-policyOn-policy: gradient needs samples from the current $\pi_\theta$ (REINFORCE, A2C; PPO is "near on-policy" via ratio+clip). Off-policy: replay old data via a bootstrapped critic (DQN, SAC).
Model-freeThe dynamics $p(s'|s,a)$ cancel out of $\nabla_\theta\log P(\tau|\theta)$ — no world model needed.
Score-function trick$\nabla_\theta P = P\,\nabla_\theta\log P$ turns an un-samplable integral into a samplable expectation.
BaselineAny action-independent $b(s)$ subtracts with zero bias (because $\mathbb{E}_a[\nabla\log\pi]=0$) and cuts variance.
Bias–variance ladderMC return (unbiased, high var) → n-step → TD(0)/bootstrapped (biased, low var); GAE's $\lambda$ interpolates.
Pseudo-lossThe policy "loss" is a scalar built so autodiff emits the PG estimator; its value is meaningless — monitor reward/KL/entropy instead. Advantages are detached.
Entropy bonus$+c\,\mathcal{H}[\pi]$ fights premature determinism / policy collapse; exploration knob.
KL penalty (RLHF)$-\beta\,\mathrm{KL}(\pi_\theta\|\pi_{\text{ref}})$ leashes the policy to the SFT reference; differentiable directly (unlike the RM reward).
Credit assignmentWhich action caused the reward? Critic/GAE = per-token credit; GRPO group mean = one credit per episode.

2 · The math walkthrough (whiteboard-ready)

2.1 Policy gradient in four lines (derive this)

$$ \nabla_\theta J = \nabla_\theta\!\int P(\tau|\theta)R(\tau)\,d\tau = \int \nabla_\theta P(\tau|\theta)\,R(\tau)\,d\tau $$ $$ = \int P(\tau|\theta)\,\nabla_\theta\log P(\tau|\theta)\,R(\tau)\,d\tau = \mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim\pi_\theta}\big[R(\tau)\,\nabla_\theta\log P(\tau|\theta)\big] $$

Key line: $\nabla_\theta P = P\,\nabla_\theta\log P$. Then expand $P(\tau|\theta)=p(s_0)\prod_t \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\,p(s_{t+1}|s_t,a_t)$ — the log turns the product into a sum and the dynamics terms have no $\theta$ and vanish:

$$ \nabla_\theta J = \mathbb{E}\Big[R(\tau)\sum_t \nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\Big] . $$

Say out loud: "the trick converts an un-samplable integral into an expectation I can Monte-Carlo with rollouts, and cancels the dynamics — that's why it's model-free."

2.2 Two variance reductions → the advantage

Causality (reward-to-go): action $a_t$ can't affect past reward, so replace $R(\tau)$ with $G_t=\sum_{t'\ge t}\gamma^{t'-t}r_{t'}$ — a single-sample estimate of $Q^\pi(s_t,a_t)$.

Baseline (3-line proof): for any $b(s)$,

$$ \mathbb{E}_{a\sim\pi}\big[\nabla_\theta\log\pi(a|s)\big] = \sum_a \pi\,\frac{\nabla_\theta\pi}{\pi} = \nabla_\theta\sum_a \pi(a|s) = \nabla_\theta 1 = 0 \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \mathbb{E}\big[\nabla_\theta\log\pi\cdot b(s)\big]=0 . $$

Best baseline ≈ $V^\pi(s)$, giving the weight $G_t - V(s_t) \approx Q - V = A(s_t,a_t)$. The general template every method instantiates:

$$ \nabla_\theta J = \mathbb{E}\big[\Psi_t\,\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\big],\qquad \Psi_t \in \{\,R(\tau),\; G_t,\; G_t-b(s_t),\; A_t,\; \text{clip-ratio}\cdot A_t\,\} $$

2.3 GAE — the $\lambda$ dial

$$ \delta_t = r_t + \gamma V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t), \qquad \hat A_t^{\text{GAE}} = \sum_{l\ge 0}(\gamma\lambda)^l\,\delta_{t+l} $$

$\lambda{=}1$ → Monte-Carlo advantage (unbiased, high variance); $\lambda{=}0$ → one-step TD (low variance, biased through $V$). Typical $\lambda=0.95$. Computed backwards in one sweep (see §7.1) — a favorite "implement this" ask.

2.4 PPO — importance sampling, then clip

Reuse a batch from $\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}$ for several gradient epochs by reweighting:

$$ L^{\text{CPI}} = \mathbb{E}_t\big[r_t(\theta)\hat A_t\big],\qquad r_t(\theta)=\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)}{\pi_{\theta_\text{old}}(a_t|s_t)} $$

Unconstrained maximization drives $r_t$ to extremes (stale advantages, IS variance) — so clip removes the incentive to leave the trust region:

$$ L^{\text{CLIP}} = \mathbb{E}_t\Big[\min\big(r_t\hat A_t,\;\text{clip}(r_t,1{-}\epsilon,1{+}\epsilon)\,\hat A_t\big)\Big] $$

Full objective (maximize): $L^{\text{CLIP}} - c_1(V_\theta - V^{\text{targ}})^2 + c_2\mathcal{H}[\pi_\theta]$ ($-\beta\,\mathrm{KL}$ to a reference in RLHF). At $\theta=\theta_\text{old}$ all ratios are 1 and $\nabla L^{\text{CLIP}} = \nabla J$ exactly.

2.5 GRPO — delete the critic

For prompts you can re-sample freely, replace the learned baseline with a group statistic. Sample $G$ responses per prompt, score with reward $r_i$:

$$ \hat A_i = \frac{r_i - \text{mean}(r_1..r_G)}{\text{std}(r_1..r_G) + \varepsilon} $$

broadcast $\hat A_i$ to every token of response $i$, and use the same clipped-ratio objective + explicit KL term. The group mean is an empirical $V(s)$ computed by actual branching — possible because an LLM prompt resets for free and the whole response is one action.

3 · Anatomy of the full RL loop

Every on-policy trainer is the same five phases; the algorithms differ only inside phases 3–4. Internalize this and you can write any of them:

# the universal on-policy skeleton
for iteration in range(N_ITERS):
    # 1. ROLLOUT   — run π_old in the env; store (s, a, logp_old, r, done, v)
    # 2. SCORE     — compute returns / advantages from the stored rewards
    #                (REINFORCE: reward-to-go; PPO: GAE; GRPO: group-normalized r)
    # 3. LOSS      — pseudo-loss on the stored batch
    #                (REINFORCE: -logp*G ; PPO/GRPO: clipped ratio*A [+VF][+ent][+KL])
    # 4. UPDATE    — one grad step (REINFORCE) or K epochs of minibatches (PPO/GRPO)
    # 5. DISCARD   — throw the batch away; fresh rollouts next iteration (on-policy!)
The two invariants to state before coding. (1) Everything computed during rollout is detached — old log-probs, rewards, advantages are constants; gradients flow only through the current policy's log-probs (and the critic's value head). (2) Phase 5 is what makes it on-policy: data is never reused across iterations, only across the K epochs within one (that's the ratio's job).

4 · REINFORCE — the full loop (the skeleton everyone must produce)

Complete and runnable on CartPole (pip install gymnasium torch). ~40 lines. This is the "can you code RL at all" question.

import gymnasium as gym
import torch, torch.nn as nn

env    = gym.make("CartPole-v1")
policy = nn.Sequential(nn.Linear(4, 128), nn.Tanh(), nn.Linear(128, 2))
opt    = torch.optim.Adam(policy.parameters(), lr=1e-2)
GAMMA  = 0.99

for episode in range(1000):
    # ---- 1. ROLLOUT: one full episode with the CURRENT policy -------------
    obs, _ = env.reset()
    logps, rewards = [], []
    done = False
    while not done:
        logits = policy(torch.as_tensor(obs, dtype=torch.float32))
        dist   = torch.distributions.Categorical(logits=logits)
        action = dist.sample()                      # stochastic! not argmax
        logps.append(dist.log_prob(action))         # keep the graph — this trains
        obs, r, term, trunc, _ = env.step(action.item())
        rewards.append(r)
        done = term or trunc

    # ---- 2. SCORE: reward-to-go, computed backwards ------------------------
    G, returns = 0.0, []
    for r in reversed(rewards):
        G = r + GAMMA * G
        returns.append(G)
    returns = torch.tensor(list(reversed(returns)))

    # normalize = crude baseline (mean) + scale control; zero bias, less variance
    returns = (returns - returns.mean()) / (returns.std() + 1e-8)

    # ---- 3. LOSS: pseudo-loss whose gradient is the PG estimator ----------
    loss = -(torch.stack(logps) * returns).sum()    # returns is detached (no graph)

    # ---- 4. UPDATE (one step)  5. DISCARD (loop makes fresh episode) ------
    opt.zero_grad(); loss.backward(); opt.step()

The five spots interviewers watch: (i) dist.sample(), not argmax — exploration; (ii) log-probs keep the graph, returns don't — the detach discipline; (iii) reward-to-go computed backwards; (iv) the minus sign (optimizers minimize, we maximize); (v) normalization stated as "zero-bias variance reduction, since a constant shift is a legal baseline."

5 · PPO — the full loop (GAE + clip + value + entropy)

The complete apparatus, still compact enough to write in a 45-minute round. Actor-critic with a shared trunk; collect a fixed horizon of steps, compute GAE, then K epochs of clipped minibatch updates.

import gymnasium as gym
import torch, torch.nn as nn

class ActorCritic(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, n_obs=4, n_act=2, h=128):
        super().__init__()
        self.trunk  = nn.Sequential(nn.Linear(n_obs, h), nn.Tanh())
        self.pi     = nn.Linear(h, n_act)           # policy head -> logits
        self.v      = nn.Linear(h, 1)               # value head  -> V(s)
    def forward(self, obs):
        z = self.trunk(obs)
        return self.pi(z), self.v(z).squeeze(-1)

def gae(rewards, values, dones, last_v, gamma=0.99, lam=0.95):
    """Backward sweep. values/rewards/dones: (T,); last_v: bootstrap for step T."""
    T   = len(rewards)
    adv = torch.zeros(T)
    a   = 0.0
    for t in reversed(range(T)):
        v_next = last_v if t == T - 1 else values[t + 1]
        nonterminal = 1.0 - dones[t]                # cut the chain at episode ends
        delta = rewards[t] + gamma * v_next * nonterminal - values[t]
        a     = delta + gamma * lam * nonterminal * a
        adv[t] = a
    returns = adv + values                          # value targets: A + V
    return adv, returns

env, net = gym.make("CartPole-v1"), ActorCritic()
opt      = torch.optim.Adam(net.parameters(), lr=3e-4)
T_HORIZON, K_EPOCHS, MB, CLIP = 2048, 10, 64, 0.2
C1_VF, C2_ENT = 0.5, 0.01
obs, _ = env.reset()

for iteration in range(200):
    # ---- 1. ROLLOUT: T steps with π_old; store everything DETACHED --------
    buf = {k: [] for k in ["obs","act","logp","rew","done","val"]}
    for _ in range(T_HORIZON):
        o = torch.as_tensor(obs, dtype=torch.float32)
        with torch.no_grad():                        # rollout never builds a graph
            logits, v = net(o)
            dist = torch.distributions.Categorical(logits=logits)
            a    = dist.sample()
            lp   = dist.log_prob(a)
        obs, r, term, trunc, _ = env.step(a.item())
        done = term or trunc
        for k, x in zip(buf, [o, a, lp, r, float(done), v]): buf[k].append(x)
        if done: obs, _ = env.reset()

    obs_b  = torch.stack(buf["obs"]);  act_b  = torch.stack(buf["act"])
    logp_b = torch.stack(buf["logp"]); val_b  = torch.stack(buf["val"])
    rew_b  = torch.tensor(buf["rew"]); done_b = torch.tensor(buf["done"])

    # ---- 2. SCORE: GAE (needs V of the state after the last step) ---------
    with torch.no_grad():
        _, last_v = net(torch.as_tensor(obs, dtype=torch.float32))
    adv_b, ret_b = gae(rew_b, val_b, done_b, last_v)
    adv_b = (adv_b - adv_b.mean()) / (adv_b.std() + 1e-8)   # whiten advantages

    # ---- 3+4. K epochs of clipped minibatch updates on the SAME batch -----
    idx = torch.arange(T_HORIZON)
    for _ in range(K_EPOCHS):
        for start in range(0, T_HORIZON, MB):
            b = idx[torch.randperm(T_HORIZON)][start:start + MB]
            logits, v = net(obs_b[b])                # CURRENT policy, with grad
            dist  = torch.distributions.Categorical(logits=logits)
            logp  = dist.log_prob(act_b[b])
            ratio = torch.exp(logp - logp_b[b])      # π_θ / π_old  (logp_b: const)

            s1 = ratio * adv_b[b]
            s2 = torch.clamp(ratio, 1 - CLIP, 1 + CLIP) * adv_b[b]
            loss_pi  = -torch.min(s1, s2).mean()     # pessimistic pair -> clip
            loss_vf  = ((v - ret_b[b]) ** 2).mean()  # the one REAL loss here
            loss_ent = -dist.entropy().mean()        # maximize entropy

            loss = loss_pi + C1_VF * loss_vf + C2_ENT * loss_ent
            opt.zero_grad(); loss.backward()
            nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(net.parameters(), 0.5)
            opt.step()
    # ---- 5. DISCARD: next iteration collects fresh data --------------------
The details that get probed, in order of likelihood. (1) torch.no_grad() around rollout — old log-probs and values are constants; forgetting this double-backprops and silently corrupts training. (2) The GAE backward sweep and the nonterminal mask that stops bootstrap across episode boundaries. (3) ratio = exp(logp − logp_old) — log-space subtraction, never a division of probabilities. (4) min(s1, s2) is the pessimistic choice — for $A>0$ it caps the upside, for $A<0$ the clipped branch caps the downside; say it. (5) Value targets are adv + values (from the old value snapshot), treated as constants — the critic regresses toward a frozen target, not a moving one. (6) Advantage whitening per batch: zero-bias (constant shift = legal baseline; scale is just LR rescaling), big stability win.

6 · GRPO for LLMs — the full loop (what a frontier-lab round actually asks)

Structure: sample a group of completions per prompt with the old policy, score them with a verifier (or RM), normalize rewards within the group, broadcast to tokens, clipped-ratio update with an explicit KL to a frozen reference. No critic anywhere. Written against a HF-style API.

import torch, torch.nn.functional as F
import copy

G, CLIP, BETA_KL, K_EPOCHS = 8, 0.2, 0.04, 2

def seq_logprobs(model, ids, mask):
    """Per-token log-probs of a sequence under `model`.
    ids: (B, L); mask: (B, L) 1 on COMPLETION tokens only (prompt & pad = 0)."""
    logits = model(ids).logits[:, :-1]               # predict token t+1 from prefix t
    logps  = torch.log_softmax(logits, dim=-1)
    token_lp = logps.gather(-1, ids[:, 1:, None]).squeeze(-1)   # (B, L-1)
    return token_lp * mask[:, 1:]                    # zero out prompt/pad positions

ref_model = copy.deepcopy(model).eval()              # frozen SFT reference
for p in ref_model.parameters(): p.requires_grad_(False)
opt = torch.optim.AdamW(model.parameters(), lr=1e-6)

for step, prompts in enumerate(prompt_loader):       # prompts: list[str], size B
    # ---- 1. ROLLOUT: G completions per prompt from π_old (no grad) --------
    with torch.no_grad():
        ids, mask = generate_group(model, prompts, n=G)   # ids: (B*G, L)
        logp_old  = seq_logprobs(model, ids, mask)        # (B*G, L-1), CONSTANT
        logp_ref  = seq_logprobs(ref_model, ids, mask)    # for the KL term

    # ---- 2. SCORE: verifier reward -> group-normalized advantage ----------
    rewards = verifier(prompts, ids)                 # (B*G,)  e.g. tests pass = 1.0
    r = rewards.view(-1, G)                          # (B, G): one group per prompt
    adv = (r - r.mean(dim=1, keepdim=True)) / (r.std(dim=1, keepdim=True) + 1e-4)
    adv = adv.view(-1, 1)                            # (B*G, 1): broadcast to tokens

    # ---- 3+4. K epochs of clipped updates on this batch -------------------
    for _ in range(K_EPOCHS):
        logp = seq_logprobs(model, ids, mask)        # CURRENT policy, with grad
        ratio = torch.exp(logp - logp_old)           # per-token IS ratio

        s1 = ratio * adv                             # adv broadcast over tokens
        s2 = torch.clamp(ratio, 1 - CLIP, 1 + CLIP) * adv
        # per-token KL to reference (k3 estimator: unbiased, low-variance, >=0)
        logr = logp_ref - logp
        kl   = torch.exp(logr) - logr - 1

        m = mask[:, 1:]                              # count only completion tokens
        per_tok = -torch.min(s1, s2) + BETA_KL * kl
        loss = (per_tok * m).sum() / m.sum()         # mean over REAL tokens only

        opt.zero_grad(); loss.backward()
        torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(model.parameters(), 1.0)
        opt.step()
    # ---- 5. DISCARD ---------------------------------------------------------
The LLM-specific traps. (1) The mask. Prompt tokens (and padding) are state, not action — no log-prob terms, no loss. Everything is multiplied by the completion mask, including the final mean. Forgetting the shift (ids[:, 1:] vs logits[:, :-1]) is the classic off-by-one. (2) Group shape discipline. Rewards reshape to (B, G) so mean/std are per-prompt, not per-batch — normalizing across different prompts mixes different difficulty levels and corrupts the advantage. (3) The KL enters the loss directly (it's analytic in θ), not through the reward — and the k3 form exp(logr) − logr − 1 is used because it's non-negative and low-variance vs the naive logp − logp_ref sample. (4) All-same-reward groups (all correct or all wrong) give std ≈ 0 → the ε guard; those groups contribute ~zero gradient — this is GRPO's known blind spot on too-easy/too-hard prompts. (5) Degenerates to REINFORCE-with-baseline when K_EPOCHS=1 (ratios ≡ 1) — good sanity answer to "what if I take one epoch?"

7 · Snippet drills (asked standalone)

7.1 GAE as a function (already in §5 — be able to write it isolated)

Signature to remember: backward loop, delta = r + γ·V(s')·(1−done) − V(s), accumulator a = delta + γλ(1−done)·a, returns adv + values.

7.2 DPO loss

def dpo_loss(logp_w, logp_l, ref_logp_w, ref_logp_l, beta=0.1):
    """All inputs: (B,) SUMMED log-probs of chosen/rejected under policy/reference."""
    margin = beta * ((logp_w - ref_logp_w) - (logp_l - ref_logp_l))
    return -F.logsigmoid(margin).mean()
# a REAL loss: fixed offline pairs, value meaningful, no sampling, no detach games.

7.3 Reward-model (Bradley–Terry) loss

def rm_loss(r_chosen, r_rejected):          # (B,) scalar rewards from the RM head
    return -F.logsigmoid(r_chosen - r_rejected).mean()

7.4 Whitening / normalize (state the justification)

adv = (adv - adv.mean()) / (adv.std() + 1e-8)
# mean-shift = legal action-independent baseline (zero bias);
# scale = effective learning-rate control. Say both halves.

7.5 Entropy of a categorical head (for the bonus term)

logp = torch.log_softmax(logits, -1)
entropy = -(logp.exp() * logp).sum(-1).mean()   # add +c2 * entropy to the objective

7.6 RLOO advantage (leave-one-out baseline)

def rloo_advantages(rewards):                 # rewards: (B, G)
    G = rewards.shape[1]
    total = rewards.sum(axis=1, keepdims=True)
    baseline = (total - rewards) / (G - 1)    # mean of the OTHER G-1 samples
    return rewards - baseline                 # == G/(G-1) * (r - group_mean)

vs GRPO, in one breath: leave-one-out makes the baseline independent of the sample it centers → strictly unbiased (GRPO's self-inclusive mean and ÷std both introduce small biases; the std term also over-weights too-easy/too-hard prompts — the Dr. GRPO fix). RLOO's original form also drops the clip: plain sequence-level REINFORCE + honest empirical baseline — the minimal member of the critic-free family. Ladder: REINFORCE → RLOO (+LOO baseline) → GRPO (+÷std, clip, KL-in-loss).

9 · What to say while coding (the narration that scores points)

  • Before writing anything: "I'll structure this as rollout → score → loss → update, and I'll keep everything from rollout detached — gradients only flow through the current policy's log-probs."
  • At the pseudo-loss: "note this loss value is meaningless — it's constructed so autodiff emits the policy-gradient estimator; I'd monitor mean reward, KL, and entropy instead."
  • At normalization: "mean subtraction is a legal baseline — zero bias since the expected score is zero — and the std division is just step-size control."
  • At the clip: "the min makes it pessimistic in both directions: for positive advantage it caps the payoff for pushing the ratio up; for negative advantage the clipped branch caps the benefit of pushing it down."
  • At GRPO's group mean: "the group mean is an empirical value baseline — I can afford it because prompts reset for free; that's exactly the branching a critic exists to amortize in classical RL."
  • If asked "why not off-policy?": "the PG expectation is under the current policy; PPO's ratio+clip only licenses a few stale epochs. True off-policy needs a bootstrapped critic — different contract (DQN/SAC)."

(4) For §6, drill the mask/shift and the (B, G) reshape — those two lines are where LLM-RL interviews are won. (5) Do the §8 table as a self-quiz: cover the right column, diagnose from the symptom.
