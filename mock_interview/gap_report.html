Meta MSL: Muse Image & Muse Video

Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL) shipped its first media-generation models — directly relevant to the vision-team interview. Official announcement → · Full deep dive →

What was announced

  • Muse Image (live) — an agentic image model, not a direct prompt→image mapper. It reasons before generating and extends the Muse Spark architecture (MSL's proprietary reasoning model, Meta's first post-Llama foundation model).
  • Tool use inside the generative loop: executes code (accurate plots, QR codes, conditioning on rendered figures) and calls web search to ground outputs in real-time facts and visual references; can hand off to Muse Spark for heavier reasoning.
  • Emergent self-refinement: during RL post-training the model learned — without being explicitly designed to — to critique its own output inside the reasoning chain, then locally edit, fully regenerate, or apply a tool.
  • Test-time compute scaling: quality improves ≈ log-linearly with inference compute; deliberate sequential reasoning beats best-of-N sampling at matched compute.
  • Editing & composition: precision single/multi-image editing, multi-reference composition with interleaved text+images.
  • Muse Video (preview) — same pretraining base, native audio; open problems Meta names: audio-video sync and physically accurate fast motion.
  • Benchmarks (Arena Elo, Jul 5 2026): #2 text-to-image, #2 single-image editing, #2 multi-image editing; Muse Video #3 text-to-video.
  • Content Seal: invisible provenance watermark robust to crop / compression / resize / screenshots, with a public detector.
  • Distribution: Meta AI app, meta.ai, Instagram Stories (US), WhatsApp; Instagram integration (reimagine photos, create with friends, personalized presets).

Test-time compute: deliberate reasoning vs best-of-N

Sequential self-refinement compounds — each step conditions on the previous attempt — so quality grows roughly Q(C) ≈ a + b·log C. Best-of-N only takes the max of independent draws, so its gains flatten (extreme-value scaling ≈ a + b·log log N).
